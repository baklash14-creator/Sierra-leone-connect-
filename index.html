About SL Connect

Built in Sierra Leone, for Sierra Leone.

SL Connect helps hard-working people find jobs and helps people who need a service find the right worker quickly. It is also a simple place to rent out a house or sell land.

Many skilled people have no easy way to be seen, and many families struggle to find someone they can trust. We close that gap by putting everyone in one place, with a direct call or WhatsApp message between them.

SL Connect

A Sierra Leonean startup that connects workers, clients, house owners and land sellers in one simple app.

Meet the founder

Founder & Developer

A young developer who loves building software. He created SL Connect to use technology to help people in Sierra Leone find work, homes and land.

🎯 Our mission

Make it easy for every worker to get work and every person to find the service or home they need.

🤝 Our values

Honesty, respect and fair opportunity for all, from Freetown to every district.

🔒 Stay safe

Always meet in person, check documents for land and houses, and never pay before you are sure.
